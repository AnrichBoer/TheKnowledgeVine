Privacy notice

Last updated 19 August 2026

Knowledge Vine Montessori respects your privacy. This notice explains how we handle information sent through this website or provided when you contact us.

Information we collect

When you use the enquiry form, we receive your name, contact number, email address and the message you provide. We may also receive basic technical information needed to keep the website secure and prevent misuse.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, arrange a visit, provide information you request and protect the website from spam or abuse. We do not sell your personal information.

Who may receive it

Information is available only to authorised Knowledge Vine Montessori staff and service providers who help operate our email and website systems. Those providers process information only to deliver their services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage any resulting relationship and meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete information that we no longer need to retain. Contact us using the details below.
